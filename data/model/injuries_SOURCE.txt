This is a headcount of listed absences, nothing more. It does not distinguish
a first-choice striker out until Christmas from a reserve full-back missing a
fortnight, and it is a single pre-season snapshot rather than a burden carried
across a campaign. Treat it as a rough signal and override by hand in
data/model/adjustments.csv where you know better.
