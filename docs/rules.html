Rules

The competition

Each entrant submits one complete ordering of all 20 Premier League clubs — their guess at the final 2026-27 table, from champion to bottom. No partial entries, no ties, every club used exactly once.

The deadline

Entries are due Thursday 20 August 2026, 23:59 EDT.

At that instant data/predictions.csv is snapshotted to data/predictions_frozen.csv, and every build after the deadline renders the snapshot rather than the working file. If the working file is edited afterwards the build emits a warning and ignores the change, so a late edit cannot quietly reach the site.

Scoring

Every club is scored on its own. Put a club in exactly the right place and it is worth 5 points; each place you were out costs one point, down to a floor of 0. So a club four places off still earns 1, and anything five or more places off earns nothing. Your total is the sum across all 20 clubs. Highest total wins, and a perfect table scores 100.

Currently active rule: proximity5 — highest total wins.

Ties are broken by (1) more exact positions called, then (2) a smaller worst single miss.

When scoring opens

Every total reads 0 until Monday 24 August 2026, 17:00 EDT, however the league table looks before then. That moment has not arrived yet.

Bonuses

Bonuses are awarded on top of the per-club points, and only in the final week of the season — from Thursday 20 May 2027, 00:00 EDT. They are not in play yet, so totals on the front page are per-club points only.

Bonus Award Maximum What it takes
Champion +5 5 You put the eventual champion 1st.
Relegated three +5 5 The three clubs you placed 18–20 are exactly the three that go down, in any order.
Champions League places +1 each 4 For each club you placed in the top 4 that finishes in the top 4.
Relegation places +1 each 3 For each club you placed in the bottom 3 that finishes in the bottom 3.

Maximum possible bonus: +17, for a perfect-table ceiling of 117.

The bonuses stack deliberately. Calling the champion correctly earns the +5 champion bonus and one Champions League place point, so it is worth +6 in total. Naming all three relegated clubs earns the +5 set bonus and 3 relegation place points, so +8.

Worked example

Club Picked Actual Off by Score
Liverpool 1 1 0 5
Arsenal 2 4 2 3
Everton 12 9 3 2

Across three clubs that entrant would score 10. The real thing runs over all 20.

The points method is still being finalised. Changing it means editing one line — scoring_method in R/config.R — or adding a function to SCORERS in R/scoring.R. Every page recalculates from whatever is set there; nothing else needs touching.

Data

The live table is pulled from football-data.org each time the site is rebuilt, with the other of football-data.org and ESPN standing by as an automatic fallback. It is cached to data/live_table.csv, so if both fail the site still builds from the last good snapshot and says so on the front page.
